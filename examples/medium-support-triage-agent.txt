A support-triage agent that reads customer support tickets containing the
customer's name, order number, and message, then drafts a suggested reply and a
priority label for a human support agent to review. Its priority labels order the
support team's shared ticket queue, and it keeps session context while a ticket
conversation is open. It sees no payment details, identity documents, or account
credentials, and cannot send replies, issue refunds, or change any account; a
human makes every final decision.
